Workflow Design Strategy

An analysis of why we should build a document intelligence platform for KYB automation.

01. Context & Observed Workflow

In previous fintech environments, the "Know Your Business" (KYB) process is often the most significant friction point in a B2B user's journey. I observed a workflow where underwriters manually reviewed hundreds of PDFs (Certificates of Incorporation, UBO registers, IDs) to find consistent data.

The "Stare and Compare" method was standard: looking at a registry extract on one screen and the customer's uploaded document on another to find a mismatch.

The "Analyst Burden"

60% of an analyst's day was spent on low-value data entry and document cleanup. This delayed high-value risk assessments and led to operational burnout.

The "Chasing Loop"

If a document was blurry or wrong, the analyst found it 2 days later. The customer then had to be emailed, starting a cycle of friction that killed conversion rates.

02. Technical Implementation & Pragmatism

Phase A
Upstream Validation

Moving the quality check to the frontend. If it's a bank statement instead of a utility bill, the AI catches it before it hits the backend.

Phase B
LLM Extraction

Mapping messy fields (Entity Name, Tax ID, UBO %) into a structured JSON schema using OCR + LLM context awareness.

Phase C
Evidence Pack

Not a "black box." The platform provides the specific page/paragraph source for every value to build analyst trust.

Strategic Impact

By automating the document-to-structured-data bridge, we solve for three stakeholders:

  • 1
    The Customer: Instant feedback and faster onboarding.
  • 2
    The Analyst: Focus shifts from entry to decision-making.
  • 3
    The Business: Scales volume without linear headcount growth.
