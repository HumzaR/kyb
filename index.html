What the platform creates

The end product is not just OCR text. It is a usable onboarding case with structured data, evidence, CRM mapping and routing.

OutputExampleWhy it matters
Company fieldsName, number, jurisdiction, addressAvoids manual copy/paste into CRM.
People and UBOsDirectors, shareholders, ownership %Makes ownership review structured.
Evidence linksDocument, page, highlighted textBuilds analyst trust and auditability.
Decision routingApprove, request info, manual reviewOnly exceptions reach analysts.
CRM schemaAuto-created properties and mappingsHelps fintechs without large ops/tooling teams.

Core product promise

The platform analyses documentation, identifies issues while the customer is still in the upload flow, and only sends clean, evidence-backed cases into the fintech’s operating systems.

✓ Faster onboarding without sacrificing checks
✓ Fewer customer chase emails
✓ Cleaner CRM/case data
✓ Better analyst confidence

Build approach

The realistic MVP is a frontend intake portal, document analysis service, rules engine, verification connectors, CRM schema mapper and review-case UI.

01

Upload intake

Dynamic checklist, file validation and customer-facing error messages.

02

Document AI

Classify, OCR, translate, extract entities and confidence score.

03

Verification

Company registry, address, ownership and customer rule checks.

04

Data model

Create/map CRM fields and relationships for companies, contacts and UBOs.

05

Review case

Evidence pack, exception summary, decision routing and audit log.

Prototype note: all uploads, extraction, verification and CRM output are simulated with mock data to demonstrate the proposed product workflow.
