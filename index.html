Task Strategy & Context

The "Behind-the-Scenes" on the workflow selection and technical pragmatism.

01. My Role & Context

In my previous role as a **Product Manager** at a commercial fintech, I oversaw the credit-decisioning engine. I observed that our biggest friction point wasn't the algorithm—it was the **unstructured data entry** performed by junior underwriters before the algorithm could even run.

02. The Workflow: "Manual Covenant Tracking"

Layperson explanation:

When a bank lends money, they set rules (covenants) like "You must always have more cash than debt." Currently, a human must manually download 12 months of bank statements, find the revenue lines in a 50-page PDF, and type them into Excel to see if the rules were broken.

The Pain: It takes 3 hours per loan. With 1,000 loans, that’s 3,000 hours of high-risk manual labor where a single typo could hide a multi-million dollar default.

03. Technical Pragmatism

  • Ingestion

    Utilizing OCR (Amazon Textract/Azure) to flatten PDFs, but adding a Coordination Layer to handle bank-specific formatting variations.

  • Intelligence

    Using an LLM (GPT-4o) with a defined JSON schema to perform Entity Extraction (identifying "Total Revenue" vs "Tax Returns").

  • Validation

    A simple Python rules-engine calculates the ratios. No "AI black box" here—the math must be deterministic for legal compliance.
