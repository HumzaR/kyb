Internal Memo: Workflow Design Strategy

Target Audience: Product & Engineering Teams

Goal: Demonstrate detail orientation, technical pragmatism, and customer empathy.

1. Role & Context

In my previous role within Fintech Operations, I noticed that the credit approval bottleneck wasn't the risk model—it was data ingestion. I observed underwriters spending 60% of their day manually copying figures from bank statements into Excel. This task focuses on automating that extraction layer.

2. The Manual Workflow (The "Pain")

The "Stare and Compare"

A borrower submits 6 months of PDFs. An analyst manually identifies "Revenue" vs. "Loan Transfers." If the analyst misreads a digit or misses a transfer, the bank funds a high-risk loan.

Current Cost: 3-4 Hours per file | High Error Rate | Stale Data

3. Technical Solution & Pragmatism

LLM-Driven Extraction

Instead of brittle regex or templates, we utilize a Large Language Model (LLM) to map fields (e.g., 'Gross Sales' or 'Incoming ACH') to a standard JSON schema, regardless of the bank's format.

Deterministic Validation

While extraction is AI-based, the math is 100% Python-based rules. This ensures legal compliance and allows analysts to "verify" rather than "data enter."
