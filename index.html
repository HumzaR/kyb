The workflow and pain

New fintechs often buy KYB tooling, but still have to stitch together document rules, CRM setup, data reconciliation, analyst queues and customer re-upload loops. The result is slow onboarding, messy HubSpot/Salesforce data, repeated customer outreach and weak auditability.

Pain 01

Document requirements vary

Different entity types, countries and risk levels need different documents, expiry rules and guardrails.

Pain 02

CRM data is not ready

Fintechs need properties, objects and associations created before extracted data can be operationalised.

Pain 03

Review is fragmented

Analysts jump between documents, registries, Sumsub, CRM tickets and spreadsheets to make one decision.

Build approach

This prototype assumes Sumsub handles the core KYB verification and document reading. The proposed product sits around it as the orchestration, CRM mapping, upload guardrail and review layer.

1. Intake

Dynamic upload checklist based on country, company type, risk appetite and customer policy.

2. Extraction

Pull extracted fields from Sumsub, OCR output or document AI. Translate where required.

3. Verification

Compare document values with registries, government sources, sanctions and CRM records.

4. Operations

Create CRM properties, case tickets, associations, audit logs and analyst review tasks.

Technical components

Config/rules engine

Defines accepted document types, expiry windows, required fields, risk triggers and routing logic.

CRM schema builder

Creates and maps company, contact, UBO, group company and ticket properties.

Verification connectors

Connects to Sumsub, Companies House or local registries and stores source-of-truth decisions.

Evidence/audit layer

Shows field-level evidence, confidence, overrides, analyst notes and final decision history.
