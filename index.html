How this would be built

This is a document intelligence workflow that analyses business documentation and turns it into operational decisions.

01

Rules engine

Accepted docs, expiry windows, required fields and routing triggers.

02

Document AI

Classifies, extracts, translates and highlights evidence.

03

Verifier

Compares extracted data with official sources and customer records.

04

CRM builder

Creates missing properties and maps extracted data.

05

Review pack

Audit trail, exceptions and next-best action.
