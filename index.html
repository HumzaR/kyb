Product / engineering pitch

Why we should build this workflow automation

This section frames the prototype against the Heron take-home brief: the observed workflow, the manual pain, the proposed automation, the MVP scope, and why it is buildable.

Workflow I observed

In a previous fintech onboarding workflow, business customers submitted company documents as part of KYB and due diligence checks. I saw the process from the product and operations side: documents were reviewed, key company/contact/ownership data was extracted, risk information was captured, and the final data had to be pushed into CRM and case-management systems.

The pain was not simply reading documents. The real problem was turning inconsistent, messy business documentation into structured, trusted, operational data that analysts and customer-facing teams could use.

Customer problem

Startup fintechs need credible KYB workflows, but often do not have large engineering or operations teams to build document intake, OCR, verification, CRM mapping, and analyst review tooling from scratch.

Wrong or poor-quality documents cause customer back-and-forth.
Analysts manually copy key fields into CRM and case tools.
Ownership and UBO structures are difficult to interpret consistently.
Teams need evidence, confidence and auditability before trusting automation.

Manual workflow before automation

1. Business applies

Customer starts onboarding and is asked to submit company documents.

2. Documents uploaded

Files may be blurry, expired, wrong, missing pages or in another language.

3. Analyst reviews

Ops/compliance manually checks validity, readability and completeness.

4. Data extracted

Company name, number, directors, UBOs and addresses are manually captured.

5. Data verified

Values are compared against registries, policies or other trusted sources.

6. CRM updated

Records, tickets and risk fields are created or updated manually.

Proposed solution

Build a KYB Document Intelligence platform that analyses business documentation directly. It validates uploads before submission, extracts and translates key fields, checks data against official sources, creates CRM-ready outputs, and produces an evidence-backed review pack for analysts.

Core idea

Move document issues as far upstream as possible, while giving analysts structured data and evidence when human review is still needed.

MVP scope

✓ Configurable document checklist
✓ Upload validation and customer-facing error messages
✓ Document classification, OCR and extraction
✓ Translation for non-English documents
✓ Registry verification and mismatch handling
✓ CRM mapping and analyst review pack

Non-goals for MVP

Do not fully automate every approval decision from day one.
Do not support every country, registry and document type immediately.
Do not replace analysts; keep them in the loop for exceptions and low-confidence cases.
Do not build heavy custom CRM implementations beyond standard mappings in the first version.

Internal ask

I would propose we build a prototype around one high-frequency workflow first: UK limited companies onboarding to a startup fintech.

The goal is to prove we can validate uploads, extract core company/UBO fields, verify against registry data, and generate a review pack that is useful enough for operations teams to trust.

If this works, we can expand by country, entity type, document type and CRM integration.

Native integrations for the systems startups already use

To work for lean fintech teams, the product should not stop at document analysis. It should push clean, structured outputs into the tools they already rely on for onboarding and operations.

Use case: startup fintechs using HubSpot as their CRM and workflow hub.

Integration value

The product becomes more valuable when startups do not have to manually re-enter extracted data. Native integrations make the output immediately operational in the CRM systems where their teams already work.

Build approach

The realistic MVP is a frontend intake portal, document analysis service, rules engine, verification connectors, CRM schema mapper and review-case UI.

01

Upload intake

Dynamic checklist, file validation and customer-facing error messages.

02

Document AI

Classify, OCR, translate, extract entities and confidence score.

03

Verification

Company registry, address, ownership and customer rule checks.

04

Data model

Create/map CRM fields and relationships for companies, contacts and UBOs.

05

Review case

Evidence pack, exception summary, decision routing and audit log.
