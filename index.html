Product Manager Take-Home Task

Project "Instant-Risk": Automating Financial Underwriting

Reducing "Time to Decision" from 4 hours to 4 minutes by automating bank statement enrichment.

01 My Role & Context

I worked as a **Product Manager** at a high-growth Fintech lender specializing in Merchant Cash Advances. My team was responsible for the "Ingestion-to-Risk" funnel. We sat between the Sales team (who wanted instant approvals) and the Credit Risk team (who required meticulous accuracy).

02 The Workflow: The "Stare and Compare"

The Process

When a small business applies for a loan, they upload 6 months of bank statements. A human Underwriter opens every PDF and manually identifies which deposits are actual revenue and which are just transfers or loans.

The Pain & Cost

Underwriters spend 70% of their day doing basic math in Excel. This creates a "bottleneck" where borrowers wait 48 hours for a decision. In fintech, speed is a feature; by the time we approve them, they've often signed with a faster competitor.

Internal Pitch: The "Instant-Risk" Engine

"Team, we are building a solution that transforms bank statements from flat images into actionable risk signals automatically."

1. Intelligent Ingestion

OCR layer that normalizes varied bank formats (Chase, Wells Fargo, etc.) into a unified JSON schema.

2. Transaction Tagging

A ML classifier that flags 'Lender-to-Lender' transfers to prevent us from funding a business that's already over-leveraged.

3. Confidence UI

A 'Human-in-the-loop' dashboard where underwriters only review transactions the AI is < 90% sure about.

Implementation Pragmatism

Component Technical Requirement MVP Approach
Data Extraction OCR & Key-Value Pair extraction. Leverage 3rd party APIs (like Heron or AWS Textract) to avoid building OCR from scratch.
Classification NLP categorization for transaction descriptions. Regex for top 20 common patterns + LLM for long-tail "messy" descriptions.
Risk Engine Automated calculation of DSCR and Net Monthly Revenue. Centralized Python rules engine to ensure math parity between AI and Analysts.

The "North Star" Metric

"Success isn't 100% automation. Success is a 50% reduction in 'Time-to-Touch' while maintaining a 0.1% error rate."
